FITNESS PLANNER — FREE 7-DAY BEGINNER GYM STARTER PLAN

Goal: Help you stop guessing what to do at the gym and build confidence safely.

DAY 1 — Full Body Intro
Warm up: 5 minutes easy cardio
Goblet Squat: 2 x 10
Machine Chest Press: 2 x 10
Lat Pulldown: 2 x 10
Leg Press: 2 x 12
Plank: 2 x 20 seconds

DAY 2 — Recovery + Confidence
Walk 15-20 minutes
Stretch hips, chest, hamstrings, and shoulders
Review gym etiquette: wipe machines, re-rack weights, avoid blocking equipment.

DAY 3 — Full Body Basics
Dumbbell Romanian Deadlift: 2 x 10
Seated Row: 2 x 10
Dumbbell Shoulder Press: 2 x 10
Leg Curl: 2 x 12
Dead Bug: 2 x 10 per side

DAY 4 — Rest
Focus on water, protein, sleep, and mobility.

DAY 5 — Full Body Repeat
Leg Press: 3 x 10
Dumbbell Bench Press: 2 x 10
Cable Row: 2 x 10
Lateral Raise: 2 x 12
Farmer Carry: 2 short walks

DAY 6 — Light Movement
Walk 20 minutes or do easy stretching.

DAY 7 — Review
Write down what felt good, what felt confusing, and what you want to improve next week.

Safety note: This is general fitness education, not medical advice. Start light and consult a professional for injuries or medical concerns.
